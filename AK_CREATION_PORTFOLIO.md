## 🛠️ Material Specifications & Engineering Standards

- **Acrylic**: 100% Virgin Cast Acrylic sheets (3mm, 5mm, 8mm, 10mm) for crystal clarity and zero yellowing under sunlight.
- **LED Modules**: IP67 waterproof injection-molded Samsung / Osram 1.2W-1.5W LED modules with lens optical beam angles of 160°.
- **Power Supply**: Rain-proof Mean Well / industrial grade 12V SMPS transformers with surge protection.
- **Metal Framework**: Zinc-chromate primer coated MS square tubes (1"x1", 1.5"x1.5", 2"x2") and 304/316 grade stainless steel.
- **ACP Panels**: 3mm & 4mm exterior grade Aluminum Composite Panels with PVDF coating for 10+ years outdoor durability.
- **Sunpack**: High-density 3mm-5mm corrugated flute virgin PP with UV fade-resistant inks.

## ⚙️ Our 4-Step Project Delivery Process

1. **Free Site Survey & Consultation**: Exact measurements, viewing distance analysis, electrical point inspection.
2. **2D & 3D Vector Design Mockup**: Digital preview showing accurate proportions, lighting colors, and scale.
3. **In-House Workshop Fabrication**: CNC routing, laser letter cut, structural welding, and 24-hour electrical stress testing.
4. **On-Site Installation & Handover**: Safe mechanical anchoring, electrical wiring, and final aesthetic inspection.
